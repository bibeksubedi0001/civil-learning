    Formulas under the hood
    • Accuracy = (TP + TN) / N
    • Precision = TP / (TP + FP) — of everything we flagged, how much was real?
    • Recall (TPR / sensitivity) = TP / (TP + FN) — of all real positives, how many did we catch?
    • Specificity = TN / (TN + FP)   FPR = 1 − Specificity
    • F1 = 2 · Precision · Recall / (Precision + Recall)
    • ROC AUC = area under the (FPR, TPR) curve — threshold-independent ranking quality.
    • Average Precision = area under the Precision–Recall curve — more informative on imbalanced datasets.
