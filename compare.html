How it's computed

$$V_{s30} = \frac{30}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{h_i}{V_{s,i}}}$$ Travel-time-weighted harmonic mean of shear-wave velocities over the top 30 m. Requires a measured Vs at every depth (downhole, crosshole, MASW or seismic CPT).

Why this comparison matters
  • The empirical formula is exact — given a complete Vs profile, NEHRP Vs30 is a definition, not an estimate. It's the ground truth.
  • But measuring Vs at depth is expensive. Seismic CPT, downhole and crosshole surveys cost thousands of dollars per borehole.
  • The ML model trades a small amount of accuracy for a huge cost saving: it predicts Vs30 from inputs you already have in any standard geotechnical log — thickness, density and soil type per layer.
  • This is the canonical civil-engineering trade-off: physics-based exactness vs data-driven cheapness. ML doesn't replace the formula — it lets you screen sites you couldn't otherwise afford to.
