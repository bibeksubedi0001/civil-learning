      What's running under the hood?
      • An MLP with a configurable stack of hidden Dense layers + chosen activation, and a final sigmoid output.
      • Real backpropagation through every layer, mini-batch SGD with the chosen batch size and learning rate, BCE loss.
      • Weights are Xavier-initialised; the training loop yields to the browser every few epochs so the canvas stays responsive.
      • The decision-boundary canvas evaluates the trained network on a 96×96 grid after each chunk — that's why busy regions get smoother as training progresses.
      • Try LR too high (≥ 1) to see the loss curve oscillate / diverge, or too low (≈ 0.001) to see painfully slow convergence — the classic hyperparameter lessons.
