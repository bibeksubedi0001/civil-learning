Phase I · Physical & Eyes (4 min)
1. Neck Rolls 1 Min
How: Sit cross-legged. Slowly roll neck clockwise (30s) then counter-clockwise (30s).
Benefit: Releases cervical tension and stimulates blood flow to the thyroid.
2. Eye Yoga (Trataka) 3 Mins
Technique 1: Focus on a distant object, then shift gaze to nose tip. Repeat.
Technique 2 (Palming): Rub palms together until warm, gently cover closed eyes.
Benefit: Relieves dryness and digital eye strain.
Phase II · Pranayama (9 min)
3. Kapalbhati 2 Mins
Skull Shining Breath: Sit straight. Take a deep inhale. Exhale forcefully through nose while pulling navel in. Inhalation happens passively.
Benefit: Detoxifies body, balances energy, stimulates metabolism.
4. Nadi Shodhana 2 Mins
Alternate Nostril: Close right nostril with thumb, inhale left. Close left, exhale right. Inhale right, exhale left.
Benefit: Balances left/right brain hemispheres, lowers cortisol.
5. Ujjayi Pranayama 3 Mins
Ocean Breath: Constrict the back of your throat slightly to create a whispering sound on inhale and exhale.
Benefit: The vibration directly massages the thyroid gland.
6. Bhramari (Bee Breath) 2 Mins
How: Close ears with thumbs, index fingers on forehead. Inhale deep, exhale while making a humming bee sound.
Benefit: Soothes nervous system via vibration.
Phase III · Dhyana & Mantra (7 min)
7. Mantra Chanting 2 Mins
Mantras: "Om Namah Shivaya" or "Gayatri Mantra".
Focus: Feel the resonance of "Om" starting in the belly and moving up to the throat.
8. Guided Visualization 5 Mins
Technique: Close eyes. Focus on breath. Visualize a bright BLUE LIGHT at the throat center, healing the gland.
Note: Let the audio guide you.
