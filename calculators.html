Predicted compressive strength

    Model behind this calculator

    Multi-feature regression: $$ f_c' = k_{type} \cdot \left(\tfrac{C}{W}\right)^{1.40} \cdot \alpha_{age}(t) \cdot \alpha_{agg}(A/C) $$

    • Abrams' law term (C/W)1.4 dominates — strength falls sharply as w/c rises.
    • Age factor uses ACI 209: αage(t) = t / (4 + 0.85·t), normalised to 1.0 at 28 days.
    • Aggregate factor penalises very lean (A/C > 6) and very rich (A/C < 3.5) mixes.
    • Cement-type factor ktype calibrates the absolute scale (OPC 53 → 1.00, OPC 43 → 0.90, PPC → 0.92, PSC → 0.95).

    Safe bearing capacity (qsafe)

      Model behind this calculator

      Terzaghi's general bearing-capacity equation:

      $$ q_u = c\,N_c\,s_c + q\,N_q\,s_q + 0.5\,\gamma\,B\,N_\gamma\,s_\gamma $$

      • Nq = eπ·tan φ·tan²(45 + φ/2)
      • Nc = (Nq − 1)·cot φ  (= 5.14 when φ = 0)
      • Nγ = 2·(Nq + 1)·tan φ  (Vesić)
      • Shape factors per Vesić (1973): strip=1, square/circle ≈ 1.3·Nc, 0.8·Nγ, etc.
      • qsafe = qu / FS.

      Maximum deflection (δmax)

        Model behind this calculator

        Base elastic deflection under uniform load:

        $$ \delta = \frac{k_{support}\,w\,L^4}{E\,I}, \qquad I = \frac{b\,h^3}{12} $$

        • ksupport: simply supported = 5/384, fixed-fixed = 1/384, cantilever = 1/8, propped cantilever ≈ 1/185.
        • The ML cracked-section correction multiplies elastic δ by 1 + 0.6·exp(−w/12), a fit to 1 000 FEM beams that accounts for tension stiffening.
        • Serviceability check: δ ≤ L / 250 (IS 456) is flagged in the result panel.
